# JLPT N5 Grammar List (Enriched)

Source: https://jlptsensei.com/jlpt-n5-grammar-list/

This is a complete list of the Japanese grammar rules needed to study for and pass the N5 Japanese Language Proficiency Test.

Each row now includes the **formation pattern**, a **usage explanation** (what it does, how it contrasts with similar points), and an **example sentence** using only N5-level vocabulary.

## Grammar Lessons (84 total)

| # | Grammar Lesson | 文法レッスン | Meaning | Structure / Formation | Usage / Explanation | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | cha ikenai / ja ikenai | ちゃいけない・じゃいけない | must not do (spoken Japanese) | Vて＋は → ちゃ; Vで＋は → じゃ. ちゃいけない / じゃいけない | Casual contraction of てはいけない (prohibition). ちゃ for 〜ては, じゃ for 〜では. Expresses "must not" in spoken speech. | ここでタバコを吸っちゃいけない。 (You mustn't smoke here.) |
| 2 | da / desu | だ / です | to be (am, is, are, were, used to) | N + だ (plain) / です (polite). Past: だった / でした. Negative: じゃない / ではありません. | The copula "to be". だ is casual, です is polite. Attaches directly to nouns and な-adjectives. | 私は学生です。 (I am a student.) 昨日は雨だった。 (It was rainy yesterday.) |
| 3 | dake | だけ | only; just; as much as ~ | N + だけ; V + だけ | Means "only / just". Emphasizes the limit or quantity. Contrast with しか〜ない (which requires a negative verb); だけ is used with affirmative. | 一つだけあります。 (There's only one.) |
| 4 | darou | だろう | I think; it seems; probably; right? | Plain sentence + だろう | Casual conjecture "probably". The plain/casual version of でしょう. Often spoken with rising intonation to ask "right?" | 明日は雨だろう。 (It will probably rain tomorrow.) |
| 5 | de | で | in; at; on; by; with; via ~ | N (place) + で; N (means) + で | Marks (1) location where an action happens, (2) means/transport (by car), (3) tool (with a pen), (4) language. Contrast with に which marks where something EXISTS. | 図書館で勉強します。 (I study at the library.) バスで行きます。 (I go by bus.) |
| 6 | demo | でも | but; however; though ~ | Sentence。でも、Sentence | "But / however" used at the start of a new sentence. Contrasts two statements. | 高いです。でも、買います。 (It's expensive. But I'll buy it.) |
| 7 | deshou | でしょう | I think; it seems; probably; right? | Plain sentence + でしょう | Polite conjecture "probably / I think". Rising intonation = seeking agreement "right?". Polite version of だろう. | 明日は晴れるでしょう。 (It will probably be sunny tomorrow.) |
| 8 | donna | どんな | what kind of; what sort of | どんな + N | Asks about the kind/type of something. Answer describes qualities. | どんな音楽が好きですか。 (What kind of music do you like?) |
| 9 | doushite | どうして | why; for what reason; how | どうして + S? | Asks "why". Also "how" in some contexts. Answers often use から (because). | どうして遅れましたか。 (Why were you late?) |
| 10 | douyatte | どうやって | how; in what way; by what means | どうやって + V? | Asks the method or means "how". Often answered with で (by means of). | どうやって学校へ行きますか。 (How do you go to school?) |
| 11 | ga | が | subject marker; however; but ~ | N + が; Clause + が + Clause | (1) Marks the grammatical subject, esp. when introducing new info. (2) "But" between two clauses (softer than けど). With 好き/嫌い/上手/下手/わかる/できる it marks the object of feeling/ability. | 猫が好きです。 (I like cats.) 高いですが、買います。 (It's expensive, but I'll buy it.) |
| 12 | ga arimasu | があります | there is; is (non-living things) | Place + に + N + が あります | States existence of inanimate objects/things. Location marked by に. Past: ありました. Negative: ありません. | 机の上に本があります。 (There's a book on the desk.) |
| 13 | ga hoshii | がほしい | to want something | N + が ほしい (です) | Expresses wanting a NOUN/thing (I want ~). For wanting to DO something, use 〜たい. ほしい is an い-adjective. | 新しい車がほしいです。 (I want a new car.) |
| 14 | ga imasu | がいます | there is; to be; is (living things) | Place + に + N + が います | States existence of living things (people/animals). Contrast with があります for objects. Past: いました. | 公園に子供がいます。 (There are children in the park.) |
| 15 | hou ga ii | ほうがいい | had better; it'd be better to; should ~ | Vた + ほうがいい; Vない + ほうがいい | Gives advice/recommendation "had better". 〜たほうがいい = should do; 〜ないほうがいい = should not do. | 早く寝たほうがいいですよ。 (You'd better sleep early.) |
| 16 | i-adjectives | い-adjectives | i-adjectives | Adj (い) + N; い→くない (neg), い→かった (past) | Adjective class ending in 〜い. Negative: drop い → くない. Past: drop い → かった. Modify nouns directly. | 高い本 (an expensive book); 高いです (it's expensive). |
| 17 | ichiban | 一番（いちばん） | the most; the best | いちばん + Adj/Adv | Superlative "the most ~". Combined with 〜の中で (among ~) for "the most within a group". | これは一番おいしいです。 (This is the most delicious.) |
| 18 | issho ni | 一緒に（いっしょに） | together | (N + と) + いっしょに + V | "Together (with)". 〜といっしょに = together with someone. | 友達といっしょに映画を見ます。 (I watch a movie with a friend.) |
| 19 | itsumo | いつも | always; usually; habitually | いつも + V (habit) | Frequency adverb "always / usually". Describes habits. | いつも七時に起きます。 (I always wake up at 7.) |
| 20 | janai / dewa nai | じゃない・ではない | to not be (am not; is not; are not) | N + じゃない (casual) / ではない (formal). Past: じゃなかった / ではなかった. | Negative copula "is not". じゃない is casual, ではない more formal. | 私は学生じゃないです。 (I am not a student.) |
| 21 | ka | か | question particle | Sentence + か | Turns a sentence into a yes/no question. Also used in embedded questions (〜かどうか = whether). | これは本ですか。 (Is this a book?) |
| 22 | ka~ka | か～か | or | A か B か | Presents two alternatives "A or B". Often followed by a question about which. | コーヒーか紅茶か、どちらにしますか。 (Coffee or tea — which will you have?) |
| 23 | kara | から | because; since; from | Reason + から + Result; Place/Time + から | (1) Marks reason/cause "because" — comes AFTER the reason clause. (2) Marks starting point in time/place "from". | 時間がないから、行きません。 (Because I have no time, I won't go.) 九時から働きます。 (I work from 9 o'clock.) |
| 24 | kata | 方（かた） | the way of doing something; how to do ~ | Vます-stem + 方（かた） | Turns a verb into "the way to do it / how to ~". e.g. 行き方 (how to go), 作り方 (how to make). | この漢字の読み方を教えてください。 (Please teach me how to read this kanji.) |
| 25 | kedo | けど | but; however; although ~ | Clause + けど + Clause | "But / although". Softer contrast than が or しかし. Also used at sentence end to soften. | 高いけど、買いました。 (It was expensive, but I bought it.) |
| 26 | keredo mo | けれども | but; however; although ~ | Clause + けれども + Clause | Formal version of けど. Same meaning "but / although". | 難しいけれども、頑張ります。 (It's difficult, but I'll do my best.) |
| 27 | mada | まだ | still; not yet | まだ + Affirmative = "still"; まだ + Negative = "not yet" | With positive verb = "still". With negative verb = "not yet". Often with 〜ていません. | まだ時間があります。 (I still have time.) まだ食べていません。 (I haven't eaten yet.) |
| 28 | mada ~te imasen | まだ～ていません | have not yet ~ | まだ + Vて + いません | "Not yet done". Emphasizes that an expected action hasn't happened. Positive counterpart: もう + past. | まだ宿題をしていません。 (I haven't done my homework yet.) |
| 29 | made | まで | until; as far as; to (an extent); even ~ | Place/Time + まで | Marks the end point of time or place "until / up to". Pairs with から (from ~ to ~). | 五時まで働きます。 (I work until 5 o'clock.) |
| 30 | mae ni | 前に（まえに） | before; in front of ~ | Vる + まえに; N + のまえに | "Before doing ~" (verb + まえに). Also "in front of" a place. Contrast with あとで (after). | 寝る前に、本を読みます。 (Before sleeping, I read a book.) |
| 31 | masen ka | ませんか | would you; do you want to; shall we ~ | Vます-stem + ませんか | Polite invitation "would you like to ~?". Negative question form that invites a yes. | いっしょに昼ご飯を食べませんか。 (Won't you have lunch with me?) |
| 32 | mashou | ましょう | let's ~; shall we ~ | Vます-stem + ましょう | Volitional "let's do ~". Proposes doing something together. | ちょっと休みましょう。 (Let's rest a bit.) |
| 33 | mashouka | ましょうか | shall I ~; used to offer help to the listener | Vます-stem + ましょうか | Offers to do something for the listener "shall I ~?". Also used to propose joint action. | 窓を開けましょうか。 (Shall I open the window?) |
| 34 | mo | も | too; also; as well | N + も | Addition particle "also / too". Replaces は/が. With negative verb = "not ~ either". | 私も行きます。 (I'll go too.) 私も行きません。 (I won't go either.) |
| 35 | mou | もう | already; anymore; again; other | もう + Past/Affirmative = "already"; もう + Negative = "not anymore"; もう一度 = "once more" | Time adverb. "Already" with completed action; "anymore" with negative; "again" in もう一度. | もう帰りました。 (I've already gone home.) もう一度言ってください。 (Please say it once more.) |
| 36 | na-adjectives | な-adjectives | na-adjectives | Nadj + な + N; Nadj + です; Nadj + に + V | Adjective class that takes な before nouns. Conjugates like nouns: じゃない (neg), だった (past). | 静かな町です。 (It's a quiet town.) |
| 37 | naa | なあ | sentence ending particle; confirmation, admiration, etc | Sentence + な / なあ | Casual sentence-final particle for reflection, admiration, or a wish. なあ adds emotional emphasis. | 今日は暑いなあ。 (Man, it's hot today.) |
| 38 | naide | ないで | without doing ~; to do [B] without doing [A] | Vない + で + [B] | "Do [B] without doing [A]". Also a way to say "don't" before ください. | 宿題をしないで、寝ました。 (I slept without doing my homework.) |
| 39 | naide kudasai | ないでください | please don't do ~ | Vない + で + ください | Polite request to NOT do something "please don't ~". | ここで写真を撮らないでください。 (Please don't take photos here.) |
| 40 | naito ikenai | ないといけない | must do; have an obligation to do ~ | Vない + といけない (いけません) | "Must do". Obligation. Literally "if I don't do, it won't be OK". Casual: なくちゃ. | 毎日漢字を勉強しないといけません。 (I have to study kanji every day.) |
| 41 | naku temo ii | なくてもいい | don't have to | Vない (→なく) + てもいい | "Don't have to / it's OK not to". Permission to NOT do something. Opposite of ないといけない. | 明日は来なくてもいいです。 (You don't have to come tomorrow.) |
| 42 | nakucha | なくちゃ | must do; need to; gotta do ~ | Vない (→なくちゃ) | Casual contraction of なくては(いけない) "gotta do". Spoken Japanese. | もう帰らなくちゃ。 (I gotta go home now.) |
| 43 | nakute wa ikenai | なくてはいけない | must do; need to do | Vない (→なくて) + はいけない | "Must do". Formal obligation. Literally "not doing is not OK". | 薬を飲まなくてはいけません。 (I must take my medicine.) |
| 44 | nakute wa naranai | なくてはならない | must do; need to do ~ | Vない (→なくて) + はならない | "Must do". Strong, formal obligation. ならない = "won't do". Similar to なくてはいけない but more emphatic/formulaic. | 学生は真面目に勉強しなくてはなりません。 (Students must study seriously.) |
| 45 | naru | なる | to become | い-adj (→く) + なる; な-adj/N + に + なる | "Become". Change of state. い-adjectives: drop い → く. な-adjectives and nouns: add に. | 寒くなりました。 (It has become cold.) 医者になりたいです。 (I want to become a doctor.) |
| 46 | ndesu | んです | to explain something; show emphasis | Plain sentence + んです | Adds explanatory emphasis "the reason is / you see". Casual contraction of のです. Gives background or seeks explanation. | 頭が痛いんです。 (It's that I have a headache.) |
| 47 | ne | ね | isn't it? right? eh? | Sentence + ね | Sentence-final particle seeking agreement/confirmation "right? / isn't it?". Softens statements. | 今日はいい天気ですね。 (Nice weather today, isn't it?) |
| 48 | ni | に | destination particle; in; at; on; to | N (time) + に; N (place) + に | Marks: (1) time of action (三時に), (2) destination of motion (学校に行く), (3) location of existence (にあります/います), (4) indirect object (にあげる). | 九時に学校に行きます。 (I go to school at 9.) |
| 49 | ni iku | に行く（にいく） | go to do ~ | Vます-stem + に + 行く/来る/帰る | Expresses purpose of motion "go/come to do ~". Purpose verb in ます-stem + に + motion verb. | スーパーへ買い物に行きます。 (I go shopping to the supermarket.) |
| 50 | ni suru | にする | to decide on | N + にする | "Decide on / choose". Used when selecting an option (e.g., ordering food). 〜にします = "I'll have ~". | コーヒーにします。 (I'll have coffee.) |
| 51 | ni/e | に/へ | to (indicates direction / destination) | Place + に / へ | Both mark destination/direction of motion verbs. へ emphasizes direction, に emphasizes the endpoint. | 日本へ行きます。 (I go to Japan.) |
| 52 | no | の | possessive particle | N1 + の + N2 | Marks possession or attribution "N1's N2". Also used to nominalize (の = "the one"), and appears in のです. | 私の本です。 (It's my book.) |
| 53 | no desu | のです | to explain something; show emphasis | Plain sentence + のです | Formal/emphatic explanation, equivalent to んです. Gives reason or context. | 今日は休みなのです。 (It's that today is a holiday.) |
| 54 | no ga heta | のが下手（のがへた） | to be bad at doing something | Vる + のが下手 (です) | "Bad at doing ~". の nominalizes the verb; 下手 = unskillful. | 料理をするのが下手です。 (I'm bad at cooking.) |
| 55 | no ga jouzu | のが上手（のがじょうず） | to be good at | Vる + のが上手 (です) | "Good at doing ~". 上手 = skillful. Opposite of 下手. | 日本語を話すのが上手です。 (She is good at speaking Japanese.) |
| 56 | no ga suki | のが好き（のがすき） | to like doing something | Vる + のが好き (です) | "Like doing ~". の turns the verb into a noun phrase; 好き = like. | 本を読むのが好きです。 (I like reading books.) |
| 57 | no naka de [A] ga ichiban | の中で[A]が一番（のなかで[A]がいちばん） | out of this group, [A] is best | Nの中 + で + N + がいちばん + Adj | Superlative within a group "among ~, ~ is the most". の中で = "within/among". | 果物の中で、りんごがいちばん好きです。 (Among fruits, I like apples the most.) |
| 58 | node | ので | because of; given that; since ~ | Reason + ので + Result; Nな + ので; Nadjな + ので | "Because / since". More polite and objective than から; gives a natural reason. | 雨なので、出かけません。 (Since it's raining, I won't go out.) |
| 59 | o / go | お / ご | polite marker; honorific prefix particle | お + 和語 (native word: お金); ご + 漢語 (Chinese-origin word: ご飯, ご家族) | Honorific/polite prefix attached to nouns. お attaches to native (和語) words, ご to Sino-Japanese (漢語) words. EXCEPTION: some 漢語 words are fixed by usage with お (お茶, お電話, お菓子) — memorize the common ones. | お茶をください。 (Tea, please.) |
| 60 | o / wo | を | object marker particle | N + を + V | Marks the direct object of a transitive verb. Also marks the path of motion verbs (道を歩く). Pronounced "o". | コーヒーを飲みます。 (I drink coffee.) |
| 61 | o kudasai | をください | please give me ~ | N + をください | Polite request for an item "please give me ~". Common when ordering/buying. | 水をください。 (Please give me water.) |
| 62 | shikashi | しかし | but; however ~ | Sentence。しかし、Sentence | Formal "but / however" at the start of a new sentence. | 安いです。しかし、あまりおいしくないです。 (It's cheap. However, it's not very tasty.) |
| 63 | sore kara | それから | and; and then; after that; since then | Sentence。それから、Sentence | Sequence connector "and then / after that". Lists actions in order. | 買い物をします。それから、家に帰ります。 (I'll shop. Then I'll go home.) |
| 64 | soshite | そして | and; and then; thus; and now ~ | Sentence。そして、Sentence | "And / and then". Adds a following action or statement. | 朝ご飯を食べます。そして、学校へ行きます。 (I eat breakfast. And then I go to school.) |
| 65 | sugiru | すぎる | too much | い-adj (→く) + すぎる; な-adj/N + すぎる; Vます-stem + すぎる | "Too much / too". Indicates excess. い-adj drop い → く; others attach directly. | この映画は長すぎます。 (This movie is too long.) |
| 66 | ta koto ga aru | たことがある | to have done something before | Vた + ことがある (ありません) | Expresses past experience "have done before". Negative: たことがない. | 京都に行ったことがあります。 (I've been to Kyoto before.) |
| 67 | tai | たい | want to do something | Vます-stem + たい (です) | "Want to do ~". Conjugates like an い-adjective. For wanting things (nouns), use がほしい. | 日本へ行きたいです。 (I want to go to Japan.) |
| 68 | tari~tari | たり～たり | do such things as A and B | Vた (→たり) + たり + する | Lists representative actions "doing things like A and B". The final verb is する. | 音楽を聞いたり、本を読んだりします。 (I do things like listen to music and read books.) |
| 69 | te aru | てある | is/has been done (resulting state) | Vて + ある | Resultant state of a deliberate action — "has been done (and the result remains)". Uses transitive verbs. | 窓が開けてあります。 (The window is open — someone opened it.) |
| 70 | te iru | ている | ongoing action or current state | Vて + いる (います) | (1) Progressive "is doing" (今〜ている). (2) Resultant state (もう食べている = has eaten). For habitual states also. | 今、音楽を聞いています。 (I'm listening to music now.) |
| 71 | te kara | てから | after doing ~ | Vて + から | "After doing ~". Sequential actions with the SAME subject. | ご飯を食べてから、散歩します。 (After eating, I take a walk.) |
| 72 | te kudasai | てください | please do | Vて + ください | Polite request/instruction "please do ~". | ここに名前を書いてください。 (Please write your name here.) |
| 73 | te wa ikenai | てはいけない | must not; may not; cannot | Vて + はいけない (いけません) | Prohibition "must not". Casual version: ちゃいけない. | ここでタバコを吸ってはいけません。 (You must not smoke here.) |
| 74 | temo ii desu | てもいいです | is OK to ~; is alright to ~; may I ~? | Vて + もいい (です) | Permission "may / it's OK to". Question form てもいいですか = "may I ~?". | ここに座ってもいいですか。 (May I sit here?) |
| 75 | to | と | and; with; as; connecting particle | N + と + N; Person + と; Quote + と + 言う | (1) "And" listing ALL items completely. (2) "With" a person. (3) Quotation marker before 言う/思う. | ペンと本を買いました。 (I bought a pen and a book.) 友達と話します。 (I talk with a friend.) |
| 76 | toki | とき | when; at this time | [Plain V/Adj/Nの] + とき | "When / at the time when ~". Present とき = general/future; past 〜たとき = when it happened. | 子どものとき、大阪に住んでいました。 (When I was a child, I lived in Osaka.) |
| 77 | totemo | とても | very; awfully; exceedingly | とても + Adj/Adv | Intensifier "very". With negative = "not very / not at all". | このケーキはとてもおいしいです。 (This cake is very delicious.) |
| 78 | tsumori | つもり | plan to ~; intend to ~ | Vる / Vない + つもり (です) | "Intend / plan to ~". States an intention. | 来週、京都へ行くつもりです。 (I plan to go to Kyoto next week.) |
| 79 | wa - topic marker | は | topic marker | N + は | Marks the TOPIC of the sentence (what the sentence is about). Pronounced "wa". Sets the context. | 私は学生です。 (As for me, I'm a student.) |
| 80 | wa ~yori... desu | は〜より・・・です | [Noun-1] is more ~ than [Noun-2] | N1 + は + N2 + より + Adj です | Comparison "A is more ~ than B". より = "than". | 日本語は英語より難しいです。 (Japanese is more difficult than English.) |
| 81 | wa dou desu ka | はどうですか | how about; how is | N + はどうですか | Asks for an opinion "how about ~ / how is ~?". | この店はどうですか。 (How is this shop?) |
| 82 | ya | や | and; or; connecting particle | N + や + N (+ など) | "And" but lists items as EXAMPLES (not exhaustive). Contrast with と (complete list). Often ends with など. | 果物や野菜などを買いました。 (I bought things like fruit and vegetables.) |
| 83 | yo | よ | you know; emphasis (ending particle) | Sentence + よ | Sentence-final particle giving new information or emphasis "you know / I tell you". | あの映画はとてもおもしろいですよ。 (That movie is really interesting, you know.) |
| 84 | yori ~hou ga | より～ほうが | [2] is more than [1] | N1 + より + N2 + のほうが + Adj | Comparison with emphasis on the preferred item "N2 is more ~ than N1". ほうが = "the one that is more". | 電車よりバスのほうが安いです。 (The bus is cheaper than the train.) |

**Note:** Officially, there are no kanji, vocabulary, or grammar lists for the JLPT. As a result, it is not possible to be certain which kanji, words, or grammar will or will not appear in a given test. Study lists are based on prior test data and comparisons with other available lists of JLPT kanji, vocabulary, grammar, etc.
